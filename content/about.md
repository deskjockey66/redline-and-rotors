title: I’m Michael.
---

I'm Michael, from Brisbane, Australia. I work as a logistics manager at a large aviation company. Before that, I spent 16 years in the Australian Army. I've got a small family with one daughter.

I got into modified cars after watching The Fast and the Furious as a teenager. My first JDM car was an R33 GTST. I still remembered how loud the aftermarket exhaust was and the turbo flutter it made. That interest eventually led me to the gunmetal grey R35 GT-R I bought in December 2025. I also own a Tesla Model Y. The white RS3 went to make room for the Nissan.

My first mechanical watch was a Seiko SKX007, which I still have. Looking at the movement through the clear caseback on mine helped get me hooked. I liked having a small machine on my wrist. These days, the Submariner has had most of my wrist time. I've also bought a Datejust 36 and a Cartier Santos, which I'll write about here.

I haven't always bought well for my own preferences. I bought a Zenith Chronomaster Sport because a Daytona was out of reach and the dealer's price appealed to me. It turned out to be too thick for my wrist, and I sold it.

I started Redline & Rotors to document the cars and watches I've bought. There's no hidden agenda. I want a record I can look back on, including the things I sold and the ones I hardly wore.

You can reach me on [Instagram, @redlinenrotors](https://www.instagram.com/redlinenrotors/).
