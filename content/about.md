I'm Michael, from Brisbane, Australia. 

I got into modified cars after watching The Fast and the Furious as a kid. My first car was an R33 GTST. I still remembered how loud the exhaust was and the turbo flutter it made. Over the years I have owned almost every JDM car except for the “exotic” types but that interest eventually led me to the gunmetal grey R35 GT-R I bought in December 2025. 

My first mechanical watch was a Seiko SKX007, which I still have. Looking at the movement through the clear caseback on mine helped get me hooked. I liked having a small machine on my wrist. These days, the Submariner has had most of my wrist time. I've also bought a Datejust 36 and a Cartier Santos, which I'll write about here.

I started Redline & Rotors to document the cars and watches I've bought. There's no hidden agenda.
