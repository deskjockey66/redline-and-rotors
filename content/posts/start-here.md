I got into modified cars after watching The Fast and the Furious as a teenager. The cars looked cool, and that interest eventually led me to an R33 GTST, my first JDM car.

I remembered the loud aftermarket exhaust and the turbo flutter. Those were the details that stuck. Years later, when I thought about how I ended up with the GT-R, the R33 still felt like the starting point.

My first mechanical watch was a Seiko SKX007. I still owned it. I remembered looking through the clear caseback on mine and watching the movement. I loved having a small machine on my wrist.

Cars and watches held my attention for the same reason. I liked their mechanical nature. The interest stayed with me even as particular cars and watches came and went.

I started this site to keep a record of that journey. Some entries would be about things I'd sold. Others would be a photograph and a short note about something I still owned. I wanted somewhere to remember what I bought and how I got on with it.
