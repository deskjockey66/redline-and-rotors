Mine was a 2024 Grand Seiko with the frost-white dial. The attention to detail drew me in. The polishing was crazy, and it was something I appreciated after buying it.

It wore well for me. I loved wearing it to the office, which was where this watch fitted into my life. That was the ownership experience I wanted to record, rather than build a story around a trip or occasion I hadn't actually described.

I eventually sold it because I was more interested in the Cartier. There wasn't a failure or a dramatic falling-out behind that decision. My interest had moved, and I made room for the watch I wanted next.

I still liked what Grand Seiko had done with the finishing. Selling it didn't change that opinion. It simply became a watch I used to own, and that was how I wanted it labelled here.
