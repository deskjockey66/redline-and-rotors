I bought the Zenith because I couldn't afford the Daytona. A dealer had this 2024 Chronomaster Sport competitively priced, with a complete set, and I went for it.

That was the honest starting point. I hadn't come to it with a long history of collecting Zenith. I was looking at chronographs, this one came up, and the deal appealed to me.

## Why it left

It was too thick for me. I didn't get on with the way it sat on my wrist, and I eventually sold it.

That was a useful lesson. I could like a watch, see value in the deal and still find that the fit stopped me enjoying ownership. The complete set didn't change how the case felt on my wrist.

I wouldn't fill out a long movement review to soften that conclusion. The reason I bought it was straightforward, and so was the reason I let it go. When I looked back at the photograph, I remembered both.
