The vintage Wolbrook shown here offers a different expression of the dive-watch idea: a compact object with a strong bezel and a dial that asks very little of its reader.

## A useful simplicity

A satisfying watch does not need to dominate the wrist. A clear face and an honest case can do the work. The pleasure is often in how the details cooperate, rather than in any single flourish.

Alongside the larger personalities of modern sports watches, older skindiver designs remind us that utility can have a lighter touch.

## A shared vocabulary

Cars and watches meet in the same appreciation for proportion, material and purpose. Neither has to be extravagant to be worth studying. Sometimes a simple object is the one that keeps drawing your eye.

The photograph is of a vintage manual-wind Wolbrook made in France. This is a design note, not a statement about its current condition or water resistance.
