My smooth-bezel Datejust was a 2025 watch and came as a complete set. The Jubilee bracelet was the part I loved most. It was divine on the wrist.

I chose the smooth bezel because it felt less flashy than the fluted version. I felt comfortable wearing it everywhere. That was my experience of this watch, rather than a promise about where anyone else should feel safe wearing theirs.

I've since bought a Datejust 36 with a fluted bezel and Jubilee bracelet. That's a separate watch, and I'll give it its own entry when I've put its photographs and details together.

For this one, I wanted to keep the note simple. The bracelet was what stayed with me. I didn't need to turn that into a long review to explain why I liked it.
