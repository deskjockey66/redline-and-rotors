I bought this Constellation from a dealer through Facebook. It was watch-only and arrived without a strap, so choosing one was part of making it mine.

I put it on a purple Delugs CTS strap. I'd also used a Delugs strap on the Submariner, and I liked the result on the Omega. It wore nicely on my wrist.

It hadn't been in regular rotation lately. That was worth recording too. I could like a watch and still leave it unworn for a while, especially in a year when the Submariner had taken most of my wrist time.

I hadn't confirmed the Omega's reference or year, so I left those details out. What I knew was how I bought it, why it ended up on that strap and how it had felt to wear. That was enough for this note.
