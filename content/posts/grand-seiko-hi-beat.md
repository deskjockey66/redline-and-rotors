I bought this vintage Seiko Hi-Beat from a dealer in Japan. The marks on the dial drew me to it. They made this particular watch feel unique, which was why I wanted it.

It hardly got worn. I liked the dial enough to buy it, but that hadn't translated into much wrist time. I wanted to keep that fact in the journal instead of write an everyday-wear story that didn't happen.

I hadn't confirmed its exact reference or service history for this entry. I left those details open rather than attach specifications from a similar-looking watch. The photograph showed the dial that interested me. My ownership note was shorter: I bought it for those marks, and it spent most of its time off my wrist.
